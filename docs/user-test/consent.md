# Consent to take part

Read this aloud to the participant, answer their questions, and ask them to sign one copy, which
you keep. They keep the other.

---

**First Paycheck user test (CS146S class project)**

You are invited to try an early version of First Paycheck, a web app that helps someone at their
first salaried job make their first-month money decisions. The session takes about 45 minutes.

**What you will do.** Answer five short questions, use the app to complete a few tasks while
thinking aloud, answer the same five questions again, and tell us what you thought.

**Only synthetic data is used.** You will sign in to a test account we created for this session,
link a pretend bank from Plaid's sandbox (no real bank is involved), and upload a made-up pay stub
we provide. Please do not type any real information about yourself, your bank, your pay or your
health coverage at any point. If you start to, we will stop you. The test account and everything in
it are deleted at the end of the session.

**What we record.** A note taker writes down what you do and say, how long each task takes and
your quiz answers. With your permission we also record the screen (not your face or voice). Notes
use a participant number, never your name. Screen recordings are deleted once the notes are
written, and no later than the end of the term.

**We are testing the app, not you.** There are no wrong answers and nothing you do is a mistake.
If something is confusing, that is exactly what we need to know.

**Taking part is voluntary.** You may skip any question or task, take a break, or stop at any time
without giving a reason, and nothing happens if you do. If you stop, we delete your notes on
request.

**Questions.** Ask the facilitator now, or email the project team at the address on the sign-up
sheet.
