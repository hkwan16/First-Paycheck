The concept

An employer match is pay you only receive if you contribute. Acme adds a dollar for every dollar you put in, up to 4% of your salary.3 Anything below 4% gives some of that pay back to Acme; anything above 4% is still worth doing, but no longer matched.

Contributions come out before federal income tax, so each dollar you defer lowers your taxable wages this year.1 In 2026 you can defer up to $24,500 in total; your 4% is well under that.2

What would change the answer

A different match formula. If Acme changed to 50% of the first 6%, the full match would need 6% from you and would be worth $1,950.3
Cash flow that cannot cover $95 a paycheck. Your October map shows about $1,619 unspent after essentials, flexible spending and savings, so it can. If essentials rise above about $3,500 a month, revisit this.
Leaving before the match vests. Acme's match vests fully after two years of service. If you expect to leave sooner, the match is worth less than the headline, though your own contributions are always yours.3
